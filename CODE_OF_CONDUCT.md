# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants should make technical collaboration safe, respectful, and evidence-led. Be specific about behavior and artifacts, separate critique of work from critique of people, acknowledge uncertainty, and give others room to correct mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate secret disclosure, sustained disruption, or knowingly presenting fabricated benchmark evidence.

## Scope

This applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project events, including private project communications used to resolve a report.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Contact the maintainer through the private route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), identify the report as conduct-related, and include only the information needed to assess it. Maintainers may warn, restrict, remove, or ban participants proportionately. They will minimize disclosure and conflicts of interest where practical.

No code of conduct can guarantee safety. This document states project expectations and the available response path.
